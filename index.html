FlowSpace: Real-Time Project Management Tool

A Jira style workspace for teams to plan sprints, assign tasks on drag-and-drop boards, and see live updates as teammates make changes. It includes activity logs, deadline tracking, and an analytics dashboard showing team velocity and progress.

HireSphere: Smart Job Portal with Applicant Tracking

A hiring platform where companies post jobs and track candidates through a Kanban style pipeline, while job seekers build profiles and apply in one click. It features resume parsing, skill-based job matching, email notifications, and role-based dashboards for employers, candidates, and admins.

Homebot System

A chat widget that answers visitors’ questions using the website’s own content, so replies are accurate and specific. It includes chat history, suggested questions, and an admin dashboard to update the knowledge base.

Book My Seat: Online Movie Ticket Booking System

“BookMySeat” is an innovative and user-friendly platform designed to streamline the process of reserving and booking movie tickets. The system aims to provide a convenient and efficient experience for movie-viewers by offering a seamless online platform that allows users to browse movie listings, select showtimes, and choose seats.
